Governance, proven

The require-tag policy flipped from audit to Deny. Same request, one tag of difference — one is blocked, one is allowed.

Blocked — no environment tag

Creating a storage account without the required tag is rejected at the control plane, before anything is provisioned.

Allowed — with the tag

The identical command, plus --tags environment=dev, sails through and provisions successfully.

  --tags environment=dev

04

What broke, and what it taught

The bugs worth keeping — the kind that only surface when you actually deploy.

Built-in policies live at tenant scope

The require-tag policy failed until its ID was resolved with tenantResourceId() instead of subscriptionResourceId() — built-in definitions aren't under the subscription path.

The Contributor GUID isn't universal

The role assignment kept failing on a "role does not exist" error — this subscription's Contributor ID ended 20f7382dd24c, not the widely documented value. Derive it, don't hard-code it.

A budget's start date is immutable

Redeploying in a new month failed because the start date had shifted. A stale budget has to be deleted with az consumption budget delete before it can be recreated.

Service CIDR must not overlap the VNet

AKS uses a separate service range (10.30.0.0/16) that deliberately avoids the VNet's 10.20.0.0/16 — overlap would break in-cluster routing.
